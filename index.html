Magnolia Research

Magnolia Research helps AI labs push their frontier model capabilities by finding where their systems fall short and building targeted post-training data to close the gap. We create high-quality reinforcement learning environments grounded in real engineering and research workflows across the AI computing stack, pairing realistic tools and verifiable outcomes so models have a place to practice complex work and labs can measure progress.
